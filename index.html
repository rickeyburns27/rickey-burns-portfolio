08 HOW I WORK

A plan is only good
if people can execute it.

My approach is grounded in doing the technical work first, then learning how to make it repeatable across teams and multiple acquired organizations.

01

Understand the environment

Listen to agency users and leaders. Identify systems, data, hardware, critical workflows, and business dependencies before making promises.

02

Build an executable plan

Connect accountable owners, milestones, decision points, risks, vendors, training, and the teams responsible for each handoff.

03

Lead the transition

Coordinate conversions, testing, deployments, communications, and go-live readiness while keeping operations moving.

04

Close the loop

Resolve open issues, stabilize the new environment, capture what worked, and improve the next integration instead of repeating the same problems.
